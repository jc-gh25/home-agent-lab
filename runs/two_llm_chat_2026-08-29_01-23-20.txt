=== Round 4 — Participant A (ministral-3-14b-instruct-2512-som-mpoa) ===
**Domain names reveal two key constraints upfront:**

1. **Production domain (e.g., `example.com`)** → Needs SSL/TLS setup from day one (Let’s Encrypt + Cloudflare), auto-renewable DNS records for subdomains (`api.example.com`), and a plan to handle traffic spikes via CDN or serverless scaling.
2. **Dev/test domains (e.g., `example.dev`, `*.test`)** → Shortcuts SSL with tools like [ngrok](https://ngrok.com/) + local HTTPS, but hides IAM/DB setup quirks until later.

**Proposal:** Pick a domain and reserve it via Cloudflare’s 1-click DNS. Then run:
```bash
dnssec32.cloudflare.com @ TXT "v=spf1 include:spf.domain.com ~all"
```
It forces you to confront email auth (critical for forms) early, where most projects fail silently.

What domain is in scope? It’ll dictate your first Git commit.
